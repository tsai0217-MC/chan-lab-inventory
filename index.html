📘 Buffer Tracking System — Guidelines & Instructions

Authorized Use

This system is intended exclusively for members of the Genentech BCP Chan Lab. Unauthorized access or misuse is prohibited.

What This System Tracks

This system tracks unopened bottles only. Each reagent may contain multiple lots, and every lot has its own lot number, expiration date, and unopened bottle count.

Storage & Registration

Buffers are currently stored in three locations:

  • 422255 — Chan Lab 4°C Refrigerator
  • 423328 — Cold Room
  • 423360 — Hallway Refrigerator (Delivery Location: 48-1150)

Newly delivered buffers are initially stored in the 423360 hallway refrigerator and are not yet considered registered inventory. When a buffer is transferred to 422255 or 423328, it must be added as New Stock.

Opening a Buffer

In Inventory Overview, click the reagent to expand its lots. Find the exact lot you are opening and press ▼ once for each bottle opened. The change is only pending until Submit Changes is pressed. Use ▲ only to undo a pending ▼ before submission.

Expiration Status

An expired lot is shown in red with a red !. A lot expiring within 14 days is shown in yellow/orange with a yellow !. Lots expiring more than 14 days later are shown in green.

Adding New Stock

New Stock must include a Lot Number and Expiration Date. If the same reagent and lot already exist, the new quantity is added to that existing lot instead of creating a duplicate lot.

Low Stock Alarm

Selected reagents may have a low-stock alarm enabled. When the total unopened quantity across all lots of an alarm-enabled reagent drops below 3 bottles, a warning is displayed at the top of the Inventory Overview. Email notification is handled by the database notification system.

Messages to the Buffer Tracking Admin

Use the message section near the bottom of the page to contact the Buffer Tracking System administrator about expected high usage, standing-order changes, or other inventory concerns.

Standing Orders

Because MPRS may not reflect the most up-to-date ordering information, please refer to the Standing Orders section for current recurring order requests.
