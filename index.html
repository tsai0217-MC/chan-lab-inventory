    ▸ Buffer Tracking System — Guidelines & Instructions

    What This System Tracks

    This system tracks unopened bottles only. Each reagent may contain multiple lots, each with its own lot number, expiration date, and unopened bottle count.

    Opening a Buffer

    Expand the reagent, identify the exact physical lot, and press ▼ once per bottle opened. The change remains pending until Submit Changes is pressed. Use ▲ only to undo a pending ▼.

    Storage & Registration

    Material delivered to the 423360 hallway refrigerator is not automatically registered inventory. Add it as New Stock when transferred into a registered storage location.

    Expiration Status

    Expired = red !, within 14 days = yellow !, more than 14 days = green.
